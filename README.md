# 🧠 Intelligent Diary

## Final Year Research Project

**Intelligent Diary** is a research-based smart diary system designed to enhance **campus student productivity** by helping students understand their daily tasks, behaviors, emotions, reflections, and productivity patterns in a meaningful and explainable way.

A normal diary mainly records what a student writes. A smart diary may provide reminders, basic tracking, or simple summaries. However, our system becomes an **Intelligent Diary** by combining four AI-based research components developed by four members.

These four research components work together to analyze diary data, identify hidden behavior patterns, improve task management, detect self-perception problems, and generate trustworthy diary summaries.

The final target of this research is to help campus students improve their **academic productivity, self-awareness, time management, decision-making, and personal well-being** through explainable and evidence-based diary intelligence.

---

## 📌 Main Research Problem

Campus students face many academic and personal challenges every day. They need to manage lectures, assignments, exams, meetings, personal tasks, social activities, stress, distractions, and emotional changes.

Many students write diaries or use productivity applications, but most existing systems only help them record information. They do not deeply analyze the student’s daily behavior or explain how those behaviors affect productivity.

Because of this, students may not clearly understand:

- Why their productivity is low
- Which habits reduce their performance
- Which activities increase stress or improve mood
- Which task should be completed first
- Whether their self-reflection is accurate
- Whether AI-generated diary summaries are based on real evidence

As a result, students may continue repeating the same unproductive behaviors without understanding the actual reasons behind them.

---

## 💡 Main Research Solution

The proposed solution is an **Intelligent Diary System** that analyzes student diary data and provides explainable, personalized, and evidence-based insights to enhance campus student productivity.

Instead of only storing diary entries, the system helps students understand their daily life by answering important questions such as:

- **What should I do first?**
- **Why am I becoming unproductive?**
- **Which behaviors repeatedly affect my mood or stress?**
- **Is my self-reflection accurate?**
- **Can I trust the AI-generated diary summary?**
- **How can I improve tomorrow?**

The system achieves this through four research components:

1. **Explainable Task Prioritization and Productivity Intelligence**
2. **Temporal Behavioral Pattern Identification**
3. **Self-Perception Bias Identification**
4. **Hallucination-Reduced Personalized Diary Summarization**

Together, these components transform a normal smart diary into an intelligent productivity assistant for campus students.

# 👥 Research Components

---

## 1. ✅ Explainable Task Prioritization and Productivity Intelligence

### 🔍 Research Problem

Campus students often have many academic and personal tasks to complete within limited time. These tasks may have different deadlines, importance levels, difficulty levels, and energy requirements.

Most task management applications allow students to create task lists, but they do not intelligently decide which task should be completed first. They also do not explain why one task is more important than another.

This creates confusion and poor task planning, which can reduce student productivity.

### 💡 Research Solution

This component provides an intelligent task prioritization method that ranks student tasks based on productivity-related factors such as urgency, importance, deadline closeness, cognitive load, and energy level.

The system does not only provide a ranked task list. It also gives clear explanations about why each task receives a specific priority level. This helps students make better decisions and understand how to manage their workload more effectively.

### 📦 What This Component Includes

- **Intelligent task ranking**
- **Explainable priority reasoning**
- **Productivity score generation**
- **Daily productivity reflection**
- **Task completion behavior analysis**
- **Next-day task planning support**

### 🎯 Expected Benefit

This component helps campus students focus on the right task at the right time. It supports better task management, reduces confusion, and improves academic productivity.

---

## 2. 🔁 Temporal Behavioral Pattern Identification

### 🔍 Research Problem

Students repeat many daily behaviors without clearly understanding their long-term effects. For example, a student may often feel stressed after meetings, become less productive at night, or feel motivated after exercise.

These repeated patterns may exist inside diary data, but normal diary applications do not automatically identify them. As a result, students cannot easily understand which behaviors are helping or harming their productivity.

### 💡 Research Solution

This component identifies repeated temporal and behavioral patterns from student diary entries. It analyzes student activities, moods, time, productivity levels, social context, and outcomes to find meaningful behavior patterns.

The system provides explainable pattern insights with supporting evidence, so students can understand why a specific pattern was identified.

### 📦 What This Component Includes

- **Repeated behavior pattern identification**
- **Time-based productivity pattern detection**
- **Mood and activity relationship analysis**
- **Stress and productivity pattern discovery**
- **Social context impact analysis**
- **Explainable pattern insights with evidence**

### 🎯 Expected Benefit

This component helps campus students identify hidden habits and repeated behaviors that affect their productivity, mood, stress, and daily performance. By understanding these patterns, students can avoid negative habits and improve positive routines.

---

## 3. 🪞 Self-Perception Bias Identification

### 🔍 Research Problem

Students may not always understand their own behavior accurately. A student may write that they studied well or stayed focused, but actual behavioral evidence may show high distraction, low educational activity, or poor focus.

This creates a mismatch between what the student believes and what actually happened.

Existing diary systems do not compare student self-reflections with objective behavioral evidence. Therefore, students may continue overestimating their productivity or underestimating their stress without realizing it.

### 💡 Research Solution

This component identifies **self-perception bias** by comparing the student’s diary claims with behavioral evidence.

It checks whether the student’s self-reported reflection matches actual activity, focus level, location, mood, health condition, and distraction behavior.

The system calculates a **Student Alignment Score** to show how closely the student’s self-reflection aligns with actual behavior.

### 📦 What This Component Includes

- **Comparison between diary claims and behavioral evidence**
- **Student Alignment Score calculation**
- **Productivity overestimation detection**
- **Focus mismatch detection**
- **Context mismatch detection**
- **Stress underestimation detection**
- **Explainable self-awareness feedback**

### 🎯 Expected Benefit

This component helps campus students improve self-awareness by showing where their personal reflection does not match their real behavior. This supports better decision-making, honest reflection, and productivity improvement.

---

## 4. 📝 Hallucination-Reduced Personalized Diary Summarization

### 🔍 Research Problem

AI-generated diary summaries can help students reflect on their daily life. However, general AI models may sometimes generate incorrect, unsupported, or fabricated information.

For example, an AI system may mention emotional states, activities, or recommendations that were not actually recorded in the student’s diary.

This is a serious issue because false diary summaries can mislead students about their own behavior, emotions, and productivity.

### 💡 Research Solution

This component generates personalized diary summaries using only real diary evidence. Instead of allowing AI to freely generate unsupported content, the system grounds each summary and recommendation in actual diary records.

This reduces hallucinations and improves trust in AI-generated diary reflections.

### 📦 What This Component Includes

- **Personalized daily and weekly diary summaries**
- **Evidence-based insight generation**
- **Traceable diary evidence**
- **Hallucination reduction**
- **Factual consistency checking**
- **Personalized productivity and well-being recommendations**

### 🎯 Expected Benefit

This component helps campus students receive accurate, trustworthy, and useful diary summaries. It ensures that AI-generated reflections are based on real student diary data instead of unsupported assumptions.

## 🔐 Ethics and Privacy Considerations

This research involves personal diary data. Therefore, privacy and ethical handling of student data are important.

The research follows these principles:

- Student participation should be voluntary
- Personal diary data should be handled securely
- Student identity should be protected
- Sensitive information should not be exposed
- AI-generated outputs should be explainable
- Facial or behavioral evidence should be used responsibly
- The system should support students, not judge them

The system is designed to improve productivity and self-awareness. It is not designed to diagnose medical, psychological, or mental health conditions.
